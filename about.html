About Us

Our History

Mangaung is a Sesotho name meaning "Place of Cheetahs," reportedly because early settlers found the plains roamed by these animals.
The municipality governs Bloemfontein and surrounding towns in South Africa's Free State province.
It has existed as a local municipality since 2000, formed by combining the former councils of Bloemfontein, Botshabelo, Thaba Nchu, and two rural councils, and became an autonomous metropolitan municipality after the 2011 local government elections.

Our Mission

Its mission centres on building prosperous, liveable, and inclusive environments supported by strong social and recreational facilities.

Our Vision

The municipality's vision describes it as a globally safe and attractive place to work, invest, and live, built on democratic governance rooted in the Constitution and working with all sectors of society to improve residents' quality of life.
